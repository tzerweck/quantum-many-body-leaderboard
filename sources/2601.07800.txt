                   We  construct  rigorous  Anderson-type  lower  bounds  on  the  ground-state  energy  of  the  spin-S
                Heisenberg antiferromagnet on the pyrochlore lattice.  By formulating and optimizing a hierarchy
                of  local  cluster  motifs  ordered  by  size,  we  generate  a  sequence  of  increasingly  tight  bounds.   A
                seven-site “hourglass” cluster composed of two corner-sharing tetrahedra furnishes an optimal lower
                bound that admits a closed-form expression for arbitrary spinS.  We also derive exact lower bounds
                for generalized models with further-neighbor exchange, ring exchange, and scalar spin-chirality in-
                teractions.  ForS= 1/2 andS= 1, numerical optimization of an 18-site “crown” cluster containing
                a hexagonal loop yields rigorous lower bounds on the ground-state energy per site of the nearest-
                neighbor Heisenberg model with unit exchange,e             GS≥−0.549832 and≥−1.632985, respectively.
                We compare the resulting bounds with numerical ground-state energy estimates from the literature.

                                                  Fermion quartets on the square lattice

                                       Dionys Baeriswyl,1  Francesco Petocchi,1, 2  and Philipp Werner1
                                    1Department of Physics,  University of Fribourg,  1700 Fribourg,  Switzerland
                          2Department of Quantum Matter Physics, University of Geneva, 1211 Geneva 4, Switzerland
                                                                    (Dated: July 25, 2023)
                    We study a microscopic model for four spinless fermions on the square lattice which exhibits a quartet bound
                 state in the strong coupling regime.  The four-particle quantum states are analyzed using symmetry arguments
                 and by introducing a zoo of relevant lattice animals.   These considerations,  as well as variational and exact
                 diagonalization calculations demonstrate the existence of a narrow quartet band at small hopping and a first
                 order transition to delocalized fermions at a critical hopping parameter, in qualitative contrast to, e. g., the BCS-
                 BEC crossover in the attractive Hubbard model. In the case of pure attraction, an intermediate phase is found, in
                 which a more extended and presumably more mobile hybrid quartet dominates the ground state.  We comment
                 on the relevance of the spin degree of freedom and on the reasons why electron quartetting is rarely observed in
                 real materials.

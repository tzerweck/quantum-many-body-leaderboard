                    We study the pyrochlore series (Eu1−xBix)2Ir2O7  for 0 ≤ x ≤  1.  We show that for small x, the lattice
                 undergoes an anomalous contraction but the all-in/all-out and metal-to-insulator transitions remain robust, and
                 the resistivity approaches a 1/T  dependence at low-T, suggesting proximity to the Weyl semimetallic phase, as
                 previously predicted theoretically.  At the boundary between Eu2Ir2O7  and Bi2Ir2O7  a qualitatively different
                 ground state emerges, which is characterized by its unusual metallic behavior and absence of magnetic ordering
                 at least down to 0.02 K.


   The 5d transition metal based oxides (TMOs) show intrigu-
ing behaviors lying outside the realm of strongly correlated
systems [1–3].  A deﬁning feature of these materials is their
relatively large spin-orbit couplingλ which competes with the
on-site Coulomb repulsionU  to give rise to a range of novel
correlated electronic phases, including quantum spin liquids,
spin-orbit  Mott  insulators,  Weyl  semimetal  and  axion  insu-
lators  [2, 4, 5].   In  the  iridates  comprising  Ir            4+  (5d5)  ions,
this interplay is further enriched as the crystal ﬁeld interac-
tion splits the t2g  orbitals to yield an effective Jeff  =  1/2
moment, which forms the basis for studying a range of exotic
quantum phenomena.
   Among iridates, the pyrochloresA2Ir2O7 (A : tri-positive
rare-earth, Y or Bi ion) are of particular interest.  The ground
state ofA2Ir2O7 is sensitive to the choice of A-cation, varying
from insulating forA = Gd−Lu and Y to metallic forA =
Pr and Bi [6,7]. The intermediate members   A = Nd, Sm and
Eu, on the other hand, show a distinct metal-to-insulator (MI)
transition concurrent with the onset of all-in/all-out (AIAO)
magnetic ordering [8–11].  For these intermediate members,

   The (Eu1−xBix)2Ir2O7 (x = 0, 0.02, 0.035, 0.05, 0.1, 0.25,
0.50, 0.75, and 1) samples were prepared using the solid-state
method.  The details of synthesis protocol are similar to that
given  in  Ref.   [20].   The  lattice  parameters  were  obtained
using  the  Rietveld  reﬁnement  done  on  very  high-resolution
synchrotron data (MCX at ELETTRA). For comparison, we
also  prepared  and  studied  homologous  (Eu1−xBix)2Sn2O7
(0 ≤ x ≤  0.1)  samples.   Magnetic  susceptibility  (χ)  and
resistivity (ρ) measurements were performed using a Physi-
cal Property Measurements System (Quantum Design). Muon
spin spectroscopy (µ+SR) measurements were done at Gen-
eral Purpose Surface-muon (GPS) and Low Temperature Fa-
cility (LTF) spectrometers at the Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI),
Switzerland.

Approximate simulation of complex quantum circuits using sparse tensors
          Benjamin N. Miller, Peter K. Elgee, Jason R. Pruitt, and Kevin C. Cox
          DEVCOM Army Research Laboratory
          2800 Powder Mill Rd, Adelphi MD 20783
          (Dated:  5 February 2026)
          The study of quantum circuit simulation using classical computers is a key research topic that helps define the
          boundary of verifiable quantum advantage, solve quantum many-body problems, and inform development of
          quantum hardware and software.  Tensor networks have become forefront mathematical tools for these tasks.
          Here we introduce a method to approximately simulate quantum circuits using sparsely-populated tensors.
          We describe a sparse tensor data structure that can represent quantum states with no underlying symmetry,
          and outline algorithms to efficiently contract and truncate these tensors.  We show that the data structure
          and contraction algorithm are efficient, leading to expected runtime scalings versus qubit number and circuit
          depth.  Our results motivate future research in optimization of sparse tensor networks for quantum simulation.

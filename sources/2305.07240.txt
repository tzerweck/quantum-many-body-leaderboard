                   We introduce a message-passing-neural-network-based wave function Ansatz to simulate extended,
                strongly interacting fermions in continuous space. Symmetry constraints, such as continuous transla-
                tion symmetries, can be readily embedded in the model.  We demonstrate its accuracy by simulating
                the ground state of the homogeneous electron gas in three spatial dimensions at different densities
                and system sizes.  With orders of magnitude fewer parameters than state-of-the-art neural-network
                wave functions, we demonstrate better or comparable ground-state energies.  Reducing the param-
                eter complexity allows scaling to N = 128 electrons, previously inaccessible to neural-network wave
                functions in continuous space, enabling future work on finite-size extrapolations to the thermody-
                namic limit.  We also show the Ansatz’s capability of quantitatively representing different phases of
                matter.

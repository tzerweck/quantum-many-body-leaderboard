               that exact autoregressive representation of a quantum state requires the virtual-bond dimension
               to scale with the amplitude mutual information. For stabilizer-state families, we show that this
               law yields an explicit, analytical rank formula.  Applying this framework across quantum-state

               neural quantum state, and the critical role of autoregressive basis ordering. These results establish
               a rigorous physical link between the intrinsic structure of a quantum many-body state and the
               corresponding neural-network capacity required for its faithful representation.

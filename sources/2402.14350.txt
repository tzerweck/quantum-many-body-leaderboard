                    The  time-evolving  matrix  product  operator  (TEMPO)  method  has  become  a  very  competitive  numerical
                 method for studying the real-time dynamics of quantum impurity problems.   For small impurities,  the most
                 challenging  calculation  in  TEMPO  is  to  construct  the  matrix  product  state  representation  of  the  Feynman-
                 Vernon  influence  functional.   In  this  work  we  propose  an  efficient  method  for  this  task,  which  exploits  the
                 time-translationally invariant property of the influence functional. The required number of matrix product state
                 multiplication in our method is almost independent of the total evolution time, as compared to the method orig-
                 inally used in TEMPO which requires a linearly scaling number of multiplications. The accuracy and efficiency
                 of this method are demonstrated for the Toulouse model and the single impurity Anderson model.

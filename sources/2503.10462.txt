                   Deep neural quantum states have recently achieved remarkable performance in solving challeng-
                ing quantum many-body problems.  While transformer networks appear particularly promising due
                to  their  success  in  computer  science,  we  show  that  previously  reported  transformer  wave  func-
                tions haven’t so far been capable to utilize their full power.  Here, we introduce the convolutional
                transformer  wave  function  (CTWF).  We  show  that  our  CTWFs  exhibit  superior  performance  in
                ground-state  search  and  non-equilibrium  dynamics  compared  to  previous  results,  demonstrating
                promising capacity in complex quantum problems.

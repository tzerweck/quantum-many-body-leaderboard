EXTENDED DATA FOR VIT CALCULATIONS OF THE J1 - J2 MODEL

In our work [arXiv:2606.04558], we have performed 1500 epochs of training with the ViT approach for each value of J2. The sample size for these calculations was set to 4096, and the neural network parameters were saved every 150 epochs. While Table I in [arXiv:2606.04558] lists the best energies obtained at these checkpoints, we complement those results in the table below with the corresponding energy errors and variances.

Meanwhile, Fig. 5 in [arXiv:2606.04558], which illustrates the V-score metric, was constructed using the network parameters from the final (1500th) epoch. The data in Fig. 5b were averaged over five independent sets of 4096 samples, generated with probability distributions encoded in the network parameters using standard NetKet functions. An example of the energy properties estimated for a single run is presented in the table below.

To confirm the stability of our results, we additionally estimated the mean energy, error, and energy variance using 102,400 samples generated from the final epoch parameters. These results are also included in the table below.

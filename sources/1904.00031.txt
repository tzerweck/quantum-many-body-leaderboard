                  We introduce NetKet, a comprehensive open source framework for the study of many-body quan-
                tum systems using machine learning techniques. The framework is built around a general and ﬂexible
                implementation of neural-network quantum states, which are used as a variational ansatz for quan-
                tum wavefunctions. NetKet provides algorithms for several key tasks in quantum many-body physics
                and quantum technology, namely quantum state tomography, supervised learning from wavefunc-
                tion data, and ground state searches for a wide range of customizable lattice models.  Our aim is
                to provide a common platform for open research and to stimulate the collaborative development of
                computational methods at the interface of machine learning and many-body physics.

             Concurrence and entanglement on a 16-site spin-1/2 pyrochlore cluster

                                                       C.  Wei∗  and  S.  H.  Curnoe†
                    Department  of  Physics  and  Physical  Oceanography,  Memorial  University  of  Newfoundland,
                                      St.   John’s,  Newfoundland  &  Labrador,  Canada  A1B  3X7
                                                         (Dated:  September 17, 2024)
                  We examine the entanglement of the ground state of a 16-site spin-1/2 pyrochlore cluster in the
                quantum spin ice regime via various calculations of I-concurrence.  Exact ground state solutions to
                a quantum spin Hamiltonian with four nearest-neighbour exchange parameters were obtained using
                exact  diagonalization.   We  present  results  for  the  ground  state  I-concurrence  in  a  region  within
                the parameter space of the model where the ground state is a singlet.  We discuss variations in the
                I-concurrence  in  the  context  of  the  composition  of  the  ground  state  and  we  demonstrate  how  a
                lattice distortion disentangles the state.

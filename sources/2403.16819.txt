                  We propose an enhanced machine learning method to calculate the ground state of two-body
               systems. By extending the original method [Naito, Naito, and Hashimoto, Phys. Rev. Research5,
               033189 (2023)], the present method enables consideration of the spin and isospin degrees of freedom
               by employing a non-fully connected deep neural network and the unsupervised machine learning
               technique.  The validity of this method is verified by calculating the unique bound state of the
               deuteron.

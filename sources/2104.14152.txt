               Stripe order in the doped Hubbard model on the honeycomb lattice

                                           Xu  Yang,1, 2  Hao  Zheng,1, 2  and  Mingpu  Qin1,∗
                     1Key  Laboratory  of  Artiﬁcial  Structures  and  Quantum  Control  (Ministry  of  Education),

                  We  study  the  ground  state  properties  of  the  doped  Hubbard  model  with  strong  interactions
                on honeycomb lattice by the Density Matrix Renormalization Group (DMRG) method.  At half-
                ﬁlling, due to the absence of minus sign problem, it is now well established by large-scale Quantum
                Monte Carlo calculations that a Dirac semi-metal to anti-ferromagnetic Mott insulator transition
                occurs with the increase of the interaction strength U for the Hubbard model on honeycomb lattice.
                However, an understanding of the fate of the anti-ferromagnetic Mott insulator when holes are doped
                into the system is still lacking.  In this work, by calculating the local spin and charge density for
                width-4 cylinders with DMRG, we discover a half-ﬁlled stripe order in the doped Hubbard model
                on  honeycomb  lattice.   We  also  perform  complementary  large-scale  mean-ﬁeld  calculations  with
                renormalized  interaction  strength.   We  observe  half-ﬁlled  stripe  order  and  ﬁnd  stripe  states  with
                ﬁlling close to one half are nearly degenerate in energy.

                   Pr2Zr2O7 is a pyrochlore quantum spin-ice candidate.  Using Raman scattering spectroscopy we
                probe crystal electric ﬁeld excitations of Pr3+, and demonstrate the importance of their interactions
                with  the  lattice.   We  identify  a  vibronic  interaction  with  a  phonon  that  leads  to  a  splitting  of  a
                doublet crystal ﬁeld excitation at around 55 meV. We also probe a splitting of the non-Kramers
                ground state doublet of Pr3+ by observing a double line of the excitations to the ﬁrst excited singlet
                stateE0g →A1g.  We show that the splitting has a strong temperature dependence, with the doublet
                structure most prominent between 50 K and 100 K, and the weight of one of the components strongly
                decreases on cooling.  We suggest a static or dynamic deviation of Pr3+  from the position in the
                ideal crystal structure can be the origin of the eﬀect, with the deviation strongly decreasing at low
                temperatures.
